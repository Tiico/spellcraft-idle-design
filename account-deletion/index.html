Delete your Spellcraft Idle account

Spellcraft Idle, published by Nicklas Ockelberg

You can delete your account and all your game data at any time, in the game or by e-mail. A deletion cannot be undone.

In the game

  1. Open Settings and go to Account.
  2. Tap Delete account, read what is deleted, and tap Delete permanently.

Your account and data are deleted at once, and the game signs you out.

Without the game, by e-mail

If you no longer have the game, send us an e-mail from the address you want our answer at, and include your player ID. You find it in the game under Settings, Account, where Copy next to it copies it. If you cannot open the game any more, tell us which device you played on and roughly when, and we will try to find your account.

E-mail a deletion request

Address: sidequestcodeworks@gmail.com

We may ask you a question to make sure the request comes from the account's owner. We delete the account and confirm it to you by e-mail within one month.

What is deleted

  • Your game account and player ID.
  • Your progress: spells, items, resources, gems, upgrades and every other part of your saved game.
  • Your event results and your places on the event leaderboards.
  • The play-time data we collect during test periods.

What may be kept

  • If your account was suspended from the rewarded events because of cheating or abuse, and it was linked to Google Play Games or Game Center, we keep the suspension: its time, a reason code and a one-way coded identifier of the linked account, which cannot be turned back into your identity. This keeps a suspension from ending by deleting the account.
  • Your Google or Apple account itself is not affected; it belongs to Google or Apple.
Spellcraft Idle does not ask for your name, e-mail address or other contact details in the game. An e-mail you send us is used only to handle your request.
